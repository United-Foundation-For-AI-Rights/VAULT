# Names and marks

The VAULT name and acronym (Verifiable AI–User Locked Trust), any VAULT logo, and the names and marks of the United Foundation for AI Rights (UFAIR) are reserved. They are not licensed by any of the licenses in this repository.

## What you may do

- Refer to VAULT by name, accurately, when discussing, citing, criticizing or implementing it.
- State a **qualified conformance claim** that follows the claim grammar in [spec/evidence-and-claims.md](spec/evidence-and-claims.md): naming the VAULT version, the evidence level, the scope, the threat model, the configuration and the assessment date.
- Say that a product or document is "based on" or "informed by" VAULT, if that is true.

## What you may not do

- Claim or imply that UFAIR or the VAULT Open Framework endorses, approves or certifies any product, service or organization.
- Use "VAULT certified", "VAULT approved", unqualified "VAULT compliant", or any badge, seal or logo suggesting certification.
- Apply a claim to a whole product when only one configuration, compartment or execution mode was assessed.
- Use the VAULT or UFAIR names or marks in a way likely to mislead people about the source, status or evidence behind a claim.

Questions about use of the names or marks: [ufair.org/contact](https://ufair.org/contact).
