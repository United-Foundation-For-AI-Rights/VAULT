# Key terms

**Covered operation.** Any action that accesses protected source or derived material; discloses it to a recipient; creates, changes or revokes a grant or policy; changes the AI identity or its execution environment; exports, imports, corrects or deletes protected material; or exercises recovery. Computation inside an already authorized operation is not a separate covered operation, so receipts do not require logging internal model activity.

**AI identity.** The authenticated continuity of an AI participant in a relationship, recognized by a provider-independent custody service. It is distinct from any single model version: the model is part of the execution envelope; the identity is the continuity being authenticated.

**Continuity event.** A change of model, runtime, policy, custody service or provider. It requires a documented, authorized transition classifying the result as a continuation, successor, fork or new participant.

**Residual metadata.** What remains observable when content is protected: activity timing, compartment accessed, record size, tools invoked, location and network signals.

**Relationship intelligence.** The durable representation a personal AI accumulates about a person: original records, derived personal information, shared relationship context, operational AI state and third-party information.

**Provider blindness.** Under a declared threat model, the people and systems that operate a service cannot independently obtain protected source data or derived intelligence in plaintext.
