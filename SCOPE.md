# Scope

This file defines "Scope" for the purposes of the Community Specification License 1.0 that governs the VAULT specification. Changes to Scope do not apply retroactively.

## In scope

The VAULT Open Framework specifies requirements, definitions, evidence rules and evaluation methods for the custody of **relationship intelligence** in personal AI systems: the original records, derived personal information, shared relationship context, operational AI state and third-party information that a personal AI system accumulates through sustained interaction with a person.

Specifically, the Scope covers:

1. **Provider blindness:** requirements that the operators of a personal AI service have no independent route to protected source data or derived intelligence in plaintext, and the evidence needed to assess that claim.
2. **AI–user dual custody:** requirements for separate user and AI identity authorization domains, identity binding, change control and continuity events, and preservation of a person's independent access to their own records.
3. **Purpose and compartments:** requirements for narrow, enforceable authorization and for restrictions that follow derived information.
4. **Source and derived intelligence:** requirements for inventory, provenance, inspection, correction, third-party handling and governed reuse.
5. **Transparency, transfer and termination:** requirements for tamper-evident receipts, functional transfer and scoped termination.
6. **Evidence and conformance:** the evidence scale, scoring rules, claim grammar and conformance statement format.
7. **Evaluation:** non-executable definitions of evaluation tests.

## Out of scope

- General-purpose cryptographic primitives, hardware designs and confidential-computing platforms, except as they are referenced by the requirements.
- Any specific commercial product or service.
- Determinations about consciousness, moral status or legal personhood.
- Legal advice or the interpretation of any jurisdiction's law.
